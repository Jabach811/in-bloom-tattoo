Quiet detail.
Plenty of soul.

Brianna is the kind of artist who notices the little things: the curve of a leaf, the shadow in an old photograph, the story behind a sketch. Her world is full of botanical forms, soft black and grey, and small details that reward a closer look.

Her approach starts with a conversation. Bring a finished idea or a handful of references; together, you find the shape, placement, and feeling that make the piece yours.
